Why a DA-trained model can forecast RT. RT and DA share the same fundamentals and average the same level over a year, so a forecast of DA is a reasonable forecast of RT. A model trained on RT learns RT's own pattern (its spikes, its typical gap to DA) but has to work from context one day staler, and RT's spikes make that context noisier.

Why the Kumo conditions might matter more for RT. RT settles on what actually happens. When load, wind or solar come in away from PJM's forecast, PJM has committed the wrong units and RT moves. The Kumo forecasts are closer to the actuals than PJM's or Meteologica's (for load and solar), and their gap to PJM's forecast is a direct estimate of that miss.
