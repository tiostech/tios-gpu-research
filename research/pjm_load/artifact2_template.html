How it works

Inputs, D−1 morningMaxar weather for 20 PJM cities; Meteologica load forecasts (ALL and 8 regions); calendar; actual load up to D−2
→
Kumo load modelContext: the last 10,000 hours of the same inputs with actual load, ending at D−2
→
Load forecast for DReplaces Meteologica ALL as the price model's load input (and the load-based stress and duck-curve features)
→
Kumo price modelProduction's inputs, last 10,000 hours of context with actual prices
→
DA energy price for DHourly median, scored on 2025

No peeking. A forecast for day D only sees what was known at 10:30 EPT on D−1. Yesterday's actual load is not complete by then, so the load model's context stops at the end of D−2, and its actual-load inputs are at least 48 hours old.

Built from forecasts made at the time. The load forecasts fed to the price model were produced day by day from October 2023 onward (a full replay, not fitted values), so every hour in the price model's context and every hour it forecasts uses a load number that could have existed on that morning.
